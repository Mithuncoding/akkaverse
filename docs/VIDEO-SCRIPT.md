# Akkaverse Hackathon Video Script

Target length: about 5 minutes.

**Before recording:** use the production build, keep the Roots demo family in its clean starting state, and pre-test the Akka question once. This build runs fully open — **there is no login** and all data is local-first on the device. Do not demonstrate cloud sync, realtime community memories, or cross-device audio until the Supabase project is restored and reconfigured.

**Tip:** speak the English narration for pacing; say the **short Kannada line (in bold)** in each section for emotional weight, or show it on screen as a caption.

## 0:00-0:35 | The problem · ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ

**On screen:** Open the Akkaverse home page. Let the bilingual EN ⇄ ಕನ್ನಡ hero settle.

**Say:**

"For millions of Kannada families living away from Karnataka, culture is not lost all at once. It fades one generation at a time. A grandparent speaks Kannada fluently, a parent understands it, and a child may know only a few words.

I built Akkaverse to change that. It is a bilingual, local-first platform that helps families preserve an elder's words and turn them into something the next generation can hear, understand, and carry forward."

**Kannada line:** **"ಭಾಷೆ ಒಮ್ಮೆಲೇ ಸಾಯುವುದಿಲ್ಲ — ಪೀಳಿಗೆ ಪೀಳಿಗೆಗೆ ಮರೆಯಾಗುತ್ತದೆ."** *(A language does not die at once — it fades generation by generation.)*

## 0:35-1:20 | Begin with your roots · ನಿಮ್ಮ ಬೇರುಗಳಿಂದ ಆರಂಭಿಸಿ

**On screen:** Select **Begin with your Roots** (**ನಿಮ್ಮ ಬೇರುಗಳಿಂದ ಆರಂಭಿಸಿ**), then **Open the letter**.

**Say:**

"Akkaverse begins with a family, not a textbook. This is my own family tree, rooted in Lakkahalli, Chikkaballapura. Every person, village, and year becomes part of a living family history.

Here, an elder's story becomes a letter to the next generation. I can listen in Kannada, and the app clearly labels this as temporary synthesized narration. It never pretends that an AI voice is my grandmother's real voice."

**On screen:** Play a few seconds of the Kannada narration. Pause speaking while it plays.

**Kannada line:** **"ಪಠ್ಯಪುಸ್ತಕದಿಂದ ಅಲ್ಲ — ಕುಟುಂಬದಿಂದ ಆರಂಭ."** *(We begin not with a textbook, but with a family.)*

## 1:20-2:10 | Preserve and pass on a voice · ಧ್ವನಿ ಪರಂಪರೆ

**On screen:** Select **Create a voice capsule** (**ಧ್ವನಿ ಸಂಪುಟ ರಚಿಸಿ**), then **Fill demo blessing**. Show the Kannada text, English meaning, family-link setting, and permission checkbox. Select **Preserve this voice**.

**Say:**

"With Voice Legacy, a family can preserve a blessing, story, song, recipe, or advice. The words are stored in Kannada and English. A family can use synthesized narration now, then attach the elder's consented original recording when it is ready.

Privacy is part of the design. I choose what can be shared, and the capsule cannot be saved until I confirm family permission."

**On screen:** Open **Child lesson** (**ಈ ಧ್ವನಿಯಿಂದ ಕಲಿಯಿರಿ**), choose the correct answer, then select **Pass it on**. Open the copied link in another tab.

**Say:**

"The same blessing becomes a small language lesson, with a phrase to repeat, useful vocabulary, and a meaning check. So a grandchild can learn Kannada from a grandparent's own words. The private link contains only this selected name, place, and text. It cannot open the sender's family tree or private recordings."

**Kannada line:** **"ಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ಆಶೀರ್ವಾದವೇ ಮೊಮ್ಮಗನ ಕನ್ನಡ ಪಾಠ."** *(Grandmother's blessing becomes the grandchild's Kannada lesson.)*

## 2:10-2:40 | Make heritage feel alive · ಜೀವಂತ ಕಥೆಗಳು

**On screen:** Open **Stories** (**ಕಥೆಗಳು**), enter **Punyakoti, the Truthful Cow**, and switch from **Cinema** to **Storybook** and **Journey**.

**Say:**

"Preservation should not feel like a database. Story Theater lets a child experience the same Kannada story as a cinematic sequence, an illustrated storybook, or a journey across Karnataka. The goal is not just to store culture, but to make a child want to explore it."

## 2:40-3:20 | Step inside Karnataka · ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಒಳಗೆ

**On screen:** Open **Explore** (**ಅನ್ವೇಷಿಸಿ**) and hover the interactive district map — click a district to reveal its live photo and summary. Cut to **Timeline** (**ಕಾಲಸರಣಿ**) and scroll the cinematic "2,500 years" chapters. Cut to **Festivals** (**ಹಬ್ಬಗಳು**) and open the immersive Mysore Dasara experience.

**Say:**

"From your family, Akkaverse opens outward into the whole culture. Explore turns Karnataka into a living map — click any of thirty districts for a photo and story. The Timeline is not a list; it is a cinematic museum you scroll through — twenty-five centuries of empires, temples, revolutions, and rockets. And Festivals put you inside the celebration, like the grandeur of Mysore Dasara."

**Kannada line:** **"ಕುಟುಂಬದಿಂದ ಇಡೀ ನಾಡಿನವರೆಗೆ — ಒಂದೇ ಪ್ರಯಾಣ."** *(From one family to the whole land — a single journey.)*

## 3:20-4:05 | Ask Akka · ಅಕ್ಕನನ್ನು ಕೇಳಿ

**On screen:** Open **Assistant** (**ಸಹಾಯಕ**), choose **ಕನ್ನಡ**, and ask: **What makes Hoysala architecture distinctive?**

**Say:**

"Akka is the AI guide across Akkaverse. I can ask in English or Kannada and choose the reply language. But this is not a generic chatbot wrapper.

Before answering, Akkaverse retrieves relevant Wikipedia material, gives it to the model as grounding, and shows the source links beside the answer. The AI key stays on the server, and common experiences still have curated fallbacks if the model or network is unavailable."

**On screen:** Point to the Kannada answer and its Wikipedia source chips.

**Kannada line:** **"ಇತಿಹಾಸವನ್ನು ಕಲ್ಪಿಸುವುದಿಲ್ಲ — ಮೂಲದಿಂದ ಹೇಳುತ್ತದೆ."** *(It does not invent history — it answers from the source.)*

## 4:05-4:30 | Resilient by design · ಸ್ಥಿತಿಸ್ಥಾಪಕ

**On screen:** Visit Roots once online, reload it, switch the browser network to offline, and reload Roots again.

**Say:**

"Akkaverse is also a Progressive Web App. Once a core family experience has been visited, it can reopen through weak or missing connectivity. Live AI and cloud narration still need a network, but the family's story does not disappear with the signal."

## 4:30-5:00 | Impact and close · ಪರಿಣಾಮ ಮತ್ತು ಮುಕ್ತಾಯ

**On screen:** Return to the home page or a closing slide with a large Kannada letter **ಕ**.

**Say:**

"Akkaverse is built with Next.js, TypeScript, a secure server-side NVIDIA NIM layer, source-grounded retrieval, and a bilingual design system. It is designed for diaspora families first, and can grow into a tool for Kannada schools, cultural chapters, and community oral-history projects.

We cannot stop the next generation from growing up far from Karnataka. But we can make sure they grow up knowing where they came from.

A language does not die when the last speaker forgets. It dies when we stop passing it on. Akkaverse is how we pass it on."

**Closing line (say in Kannada, then English):** **"ನಮ್ಮ ಕಥೆ ಮುಂದುವರಿಯಲಿ."** *"Let our story continue."*
