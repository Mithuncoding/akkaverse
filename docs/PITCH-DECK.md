## Slide 1 — Title

*The living universe of Kannada heritage.*
- Tagline: *Preserve a voice. Pass on a language.*
- Your name / team, Devfolio, date, live demo URL.
- **Visual:** the bilingual home hero + logo.

## Slide 2 — The Problem
- Kannada culture fades **one generation at a time**: grandparent speaks it,
  parent understands it, child knows a few words.
- When an elder passes, their **voice, blessings, and stories are lost**.
- Existing heritage apps are **static encyclopedias** — they store facts, not
  living family memory.
- **Visual:** 3-generation "fluent → understands → few words" graphic.

## Slide 3 — The Solution
- Akkaverse starts with **your family, not a textbook**, then expands outward
  into the whole culture.
- Three pillars: **Preserve** (Voice Legacy) · **Understand** (grounded AI) ·
  **Explore** (maps, timeline, festivals).
- Fully **bilingual** and **local-first (no login)**.
- **Visual:** the "Roots → Land → Stories → Festivals" journey strip.

## Slide 4 — Live Demo (the hero moment)
- Screenshot/GIF of **Voice Legacy**: preserve an elder's blessing → it becomes
  a **Kannada lesson** (repeat a phrase, vocabulary, meaning check).
- One line: *"A grandmother's blessing becomes the grandchild's Kannada lesson."*
- **Visual:** the capsule card + "Learn from this voice" panel.

## Slide 5 — Ask Akka: grounded AI
- Not a chatbot wrapper: **retrieves Wikipedia → grounds the model → shows
  citations**, in English or Kannada.
- Server-only NVIDIA key; curated fallbacks when offline.
- **Visual:** a Kannada answer with Wikipedia source chips.

## Slide 6 — Experience the culture
- **Explore** (interactive district map), **Timeline** (cinematic 2,500 years),
  **Festivals** (immersive Dasara), **Stories**, **Learn**, **Quiz**.
- **Visual:** 2×2 grid of these screens.

## Slide 7 — Technical Architecture
- Next.js + NVIDIA NIM (Llama 3.1 8B → Nemotron 49B) + Wikipedia grounding.
- Local-first: `localStorage` + IndexedDB; offline PWA; secrets server-side.
- **Visual:** the architecture diagram (from README / SUBMISSION.md).

## Slide 8 — Business Viability & Scale
- **Cheap to run:** one Vercel deploy + one free NVIDIA key; no DB/auth infra.
- **Scale paths:** Karnataka **tourism** (district guides), **schools**
  (teacher mode), **cultural associations** & diaspora chapters, oral-history
  archives. Same engine, new content packs.
- **Visual:** funnel — Families → Schools → Tourism/Govt/NGO.

## Slide 9 — Impact & Feedback
- Turns "someday I'll record grandpa" into a **1-minute preserve-and-teach loop**.
- **Privacy-first & inclusive:** no account, no upload, works offline — usable by
  non-technical elders.
- Add any **testing/feedback** gathered during the build phase here.
- **Visual:** before/after or a short testimonial quote.

## Slide 10 — Roadmap & Ask
- Next 6 months: **voice cloning** (narrate new text in the elder's own voice,
  with consent), **opt-in cloud sync**, **community wall with moderation**,
  **school mode**, richer Kannada-literature grounding.
- Close: *"A language dies when we stop passing it on. Akkaverse is how we pass
  it on."* · **ನಮ್ಮ ಕಥೆ ಮುಂದುವರಿಯಲಿ.**
- Repo + live demo links + contact.
